> The sky appears blue due to a phenomenon called **Rayleigh scattering**. As sunlight travels through Earth's atmosphere, it encounters gas molecules like nitrogen and oxygen. These molecules are much smaller than the wavelengths of visible light, causing them to scatter the light in all directions.
> 
> The wavelength of blue light is shorter than that of red light. > 
> During the day, this scattered blue light dominates our vision. However, during sunrise or sunset, sunlight travels through a thicker layer of atmosphere, scattering away most of the blue light before it reaches your eyes, leaving the longer wavelengths of red and orange to dominate the horizon.
